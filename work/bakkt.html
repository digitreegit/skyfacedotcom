Client · Web · Mobile app

Bakkt

UI/UX mockup and prototype for buying, selling, storing, and sending digital assets on web and mobile.

Platforms
Web app, mobile app
Scope
UI/UX, mockup, prototype
Focus
UI/UX, Design System

Bakkt’s brief was a trusted digital-asset platform: buy, sell, store, and spend in one product, on web and on the phone. These screens are the UI/UX mockup and prototype for that flow.

Trade at a glance

Price, balance, and a buy or sell ticket sit on one home – chart first, then the action.

Send and request

P2P transfers with amount, message, and a wallet QR so an address can be shared or copied.

Spend in the world

Linked banks and merchants, including a Starbucks card top-up, so digital assets can leave the app.
