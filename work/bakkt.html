Bakkt builds next-generation digital payments and financial infrastructure – trading, money movement, and programmable money across digital assets. Built to scale. Engineered for security. Designed for compliance. These screens are the UI/UX mockup and prototype for that story: a trusted platform to buy, sell, store, and spend, on web and on the phone.

Digital asset trading

Price, balance, and a buy or sell ticket sit on one home – chart first, then the action.

Money movement, on demand

Designed to spend

Linked banks and merchants, including a Starbucks card, so digital assets can leave the wallet.
