My Password Vault

Spreadsheet-like password manager. Intuitive and secure. Syncs across devices.

Visit product

Design & development
Focus
HCI, UI/UX

My Password Vault is a password manager that feels as simple as a spreadsheet – easy to scan, easy to edit, and intuitive from day one.

Spreadsheet-simple

See logins in a clean table: site, username, password, notes, and categories. Add a row, fill it in, find it fast.

Secure without friction

Encrypted on-device. Unlock with a passkey, with TOTP and recovery codes as backup. Only ciphertext syncs to the server.

Free, then one payment

Start with 25 entries. Unlock unlimited with a one-time Pro license. No subscription.

Easy and intuitive

  • Quick search and categories to stay organized
  • Copy passwords when you need them
  • Works on phone and web with the same account
  • Free to start – upgrade only when you need more entries

Privacy first

No ads in the vault. We do not sell personal information. Sign in with Google, Apple, or email.
